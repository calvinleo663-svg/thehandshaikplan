What I do today

Signal Finder, built with Claude

Pulls fresh funding rounds from public sources, scores each company out of 10 on size, stage, recency and fit, and ranks who to call first. It took me a day, and it does a small slice of what Handshaik does.

What I'd do with Handshaik

Brief in, ranked list out, then the phone

Set a brief per segment, use the fit explanation and company signals to write the opener, enrich the right contact, and work the list in a shared pipeline the Founder and Sales Lead can see. Anything that slows me down goes to product as feedback.

    The three phases

    Days 1 to 30 Find.

    Learn the product, the buyer and what a good meeting looks like

    • Learn Handshaik well enough to demo the basics: briefs, company profiles, signals, contact enrichment and the pipeline.
    • Learn how dealmakers work: M&A advisers, corporate finance, PE and corp dev, accountancy and consulting BD teams, and B2B sales teams.
    • Listen to the Founder's and Sales Lead's calls, read customer conversations, and agree what a qualified meeting means here.
    • Build my first lists in Handshaik by segment and start calling in week two, with feedback on every early conversation.
    Day 30 check-inI can explain Handshaik in one sentence to each segment, I'm on the phone daily, and my CRM notes pass review.
    Days 31 to 60 Nurture.

    Full daily rhythm and the first meetings booked

    • Steady daily calling to the right people, with personalised email and LinkedIn around each call.
    • Every account researched before I dial: fit reason, recent signal, who decides.
    • Book meetings for the Sales Lead with the full handover note, and follow up every "not yet" with a date and a reason.
    • Log every call and outcome the same day so the pipeline is always true.
    Day 60 check-inA steady rhythm, meetings the Sales Lead is glad to take, and a clean pipeline.
    Days 61 to 90 Win.

    Test what works, and start adding to marketing

    • Run small tests on segments and openers (for example accountancy BD vs. M&A advisers) and keep the winners.
    • Write openers by signal: a firm hiring BD people, a new partner, a fund or adviser moving into a new sector.
    • Share a short weekly note with sales, marketing and product on what buyers are saying.
    • Turn what I learn on calls into LinkedIn posts with the Founder, and sit in on demos to start the path to Account Executive.
    Day 90 check-inTests run and written up, pipeline growing, and a plan for next quarter.
